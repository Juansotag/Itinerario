## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intimate, evocative narrative for a surprise romantic voyage. It captures the sultry golden hour of Barranquilla—where the Magdalena River meets the Caribbean Sea—merging tropical coastal warmth with classic editorial romance.

The aesthetic avoids sterile modern SaaS conventions, tech-heavy aesthetics, and corporate predictability. Instead, it draws from:
- **Warm Editorial Narrative:** High-contrast romantic serif headlines evoking bespoke travel stationery, love letters, and keepsakes.
- **Tactile Linen & Paper Metaphor:** Textured creamy canvases, stitched accents, delicate debossed card contours, and sealed envelope motifs.
- **Sun-Drenched Color Geometry:** Terracotta, coral glow, and deep vintage wine accents layered over breathable linen surfaces.
- **Mysterious Nocturne Layers:** Deep twilight slates reserved exclusively for veiled itineraries, locked future days, and surprise moments waiting to be revealed.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the transition from sunlit Caribbean architecture to romantic coastal dusk:

- **Primary (`#D96B43` - Caribbean Terracotta):** Used for primary calls to action, active navigation anchors, and focal route highlights.
- **Secondary (`#E88D72` - Warm Coral Blush):** Used for supporting visual tags, romantic micro-accents, tender highlights, and interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#871D2D` - Passion Wine):** Reserved for high-emotion focal points: countdown highlights, sealed secret badges, love notes, and key romantic markers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF6F0` - Linen Cream):** Primary application backdrop providing natural warmth without eye fatigue. Accompanied by `#F3EDE2` (Sand Dune) for card surfaces and subtle borders.
- **Mystery & Slate Accents (`#1C1917` & `#2A2421`):** Deep charcoal slate reserved for surprise envelopes, locked activity stages, night-mode schedules, and high-contrast editorial typography.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Deep slate `#2A2421` serves as the primary ink tone; `#786B65` serves as the muted secondary text tone. Raw `#000000` is strictly avoided to preserve the organic paper feel.

## Typography

The type system balances evocative editorial warmth with functional utility:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the grace of hand-bound travel journals, boutique hotel stationary, and personal love letters. Use italic styling sparingly for poetic micro-copy, quotes, or location sub-labels (e.g., *Barranquilla, Colombia*).
- **Body & Data Displays (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides geometric legibility for flight numbers, logistics, time markers, addresses, and daily scheduling.
- **Numbers & Coordinates:** Itinerary timestamps, flight gates, and countdown timers use `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular numeric lining for visual order.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first architecture optimized for single-handed thumb interaction during transit, expanding into an elegant centered reading view on larger screens.

- **Mobile Canvas (<768px):** Single-column timeline feed bounded by a `1.25rem` outer margin. Touch targets for itinerary toggles and expandable secret cards maintain a minimum 48px footprint.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column fluid structure with a 48rem max-width container, allowing photos and itinerary notes to sit side-by-side.
- **Desktop (>=1024px):** Centered presentation stage capped at 72rem with a 12-column layout. Keeps the focus centered like an open two-page travel album.
- **Rhythm:** Layout follows a strict 4px/8px rhythm. Component internal padding leans generous (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to preserve the leisurely, unhurried atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile, warm ambient shadows rather than synthetic digital blurs:

- **Natural Sunken Level (Base):** Linen cream canvas (`#FAF6F0`) resting flat under the visual hierarchy.
- **Paper Sheet Elevation:** Itinerary cards and note blocks sit on `#F3EDE2` with a feathered warm drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(135, 29, 45, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Raised Interactive Cards:** Tappable secret envelopes and interactive map modules lift on hover or touch with `0 14px 32px -6px rgba(217, 107, 67, 0.16)`.
- **Veiled Nocturne Layers (Locked Surprises):** Locked activities feature a layered dark slate treatment (`#1C1917`) with a delicate inset warm border (`rgba(232, 141, 114, 0.15)`) to communicate curiosity, intimacy, and mystery.
- **No Harsh Outlines:** Never use solid 1px black borders. Rely instead on soft tone shifts (`#E8DFC8`) or tone-on-tone hairline borders (`rgba(217, 107, 67, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape system expresses soft organic warmth while preserving structured readability:

- **Cards & Surfaces:** Built with 16px to 24px border radii (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), creating the feeling of smooth, pebble-rounded deckle-edge paper.
- **Pills & Badges:** Sealed mystery badges, time stamps, and category pills employ full pill rounding (`9999px`).
- **Wax Seals & Medallions:** Secret reveals and countdown icons use circular containers with soft serrated or stamped contours.
- **Tear-Off Dividers:** Dashed border strokes (`2px dashed #DCCFBF`) emulate vintage flight coupons and keepsake ticket stubs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Unveil the Secret" / "Confirm Flight"):** Solid terracotta (`#D96B43`) background with white typography, 16px radius, cushioned with `space-md` horizontal padding. Subtle warmth glow on active state.
- **Romantic Tertiary ("Read Love Note"):** Deep wine (`#871D2D`) background, creamy linen text, paired with a delicate vector ribbon or heart icon.
- **Ghost/Outline:** Sand-toned border (`1.5px solid #E88D72`), terracotta label, transparent fill.

### Cards (Day-by-Day Itineraries)
- Grounded in `#F3EDE2` with 20px corners and warm ambient shadow.
- Header holds a vector compass or route mark alongside the timestamp.
- **Locked/Surprise State:** Deep twilight slate container (`#1C1917`) with gold/coral wax seal button and mystery prompt (*"Revelación a las 7:00 PM"*).

### Chips & Badges
- Time chips: Linen tone (`#FAF6F0`) with terracotta text and subtle coral hairline border.
- Mood tags: Warm blush coral (`#E88D72`) at 15% opacity with dark wine text (`#871D2D`).

### Lists & Timeline Trackers
- A subtle dashed terracotta line connects time slots along the vertical scroll.
- Timeline checkpoints use custom illustrated vector badges (sun, paper plane, wine glasses, tropical palm) instead of standard bullet points.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Understated linen background (`#FAF6F0`) with soft inset sand shading, deep slate text (`#2A2421`), and a coral focus ring (`2px solid #E88D72`).

### Iconography Style
- Exclusively bespoke line vectors with 1.5px warm stroke weights and organic rounded terminals.
- Themes: Wax seals, paper airplanes, antique compass stars, tropical botanicals, and heart seals. Standard operating system emojis and flat tech iconography are strictly forbidden.
